Effect Size Finder

Find the published effect sizes a study plan needs

Describe the study you are planning. Claude, or an OpenAI model if you prefer, searches PubMed, Europe PMC and ClinicalTrials.gov, reads the papers it can open, and pulls out the statistics they report, each with the sentence or table row it came from. You decide what to include, and Opus 5.5 summarizes what the papers you kept report. Everything runs in this page, with your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key.

  • Searches the literature

    From the top down: reviews and meta-analyses first, then studies in your population, then papers whose full text reports the outcome (Europe PMC), then wider, plus the trials the reviews cite. Also results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov.

  • Every number is quoted

    Each value carries its quote, which is checked against the paper. Anything that fails a check goes to a second, stronger review.

  • You decide

    Include or exclude each result, then export what you kept as CSV or JSON.

Set up

Everything runs in this page with your own API key, from Anthropic for Claude or from OpenAI. The contact email and NCBI key are optional, but they help the search.

Models

OpenAI has nothing like Claude's web fetch, so a PDF this page may not download is left for you to upload. To run on your ChatGPT plan instead of a key, use the local edition: OpenAI allows Sign in with ChatGPT only for apps that run on your own computer.

Who does what

    Your key, searches and decisions stay in this browser. Your paragraph and the papers' text are sent to Anthropic's API to be read, and searches go to PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, Europe PMC and Unpaywall. Don't paste anything you can't share with them.

    Describe the study
