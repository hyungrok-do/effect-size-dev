Effect Size Finder

Find the published numbers a study plan needs

Describe the study you are planning. Claude searches PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov, reads the papers it can open, and pulls out the statistics they report, each with the sentence or table row it came from. You decide what to include. Everything runs in this page, with your own Anthropic API key.

  • Searches the literature

    PubMed, results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov, and open-access full text from PubMed Central and Unpaywall. PDFs the browser can't download, Claude fetches.

  • Every number is quoted

    Each value carries its quote, which is checked against the paper. Anything that fails a check goes to a second, stronger review.

  • You decide

    Include or exclude each result, then export what you kept as CSV or JSON.

Your key, searches and decisions stay in this browser. Your paragraph and the papers' text are sent to Anthropic's API to be read, and searches go to PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, Europe PMC and Unpaywall. Don't paste anything you can't share with them.
